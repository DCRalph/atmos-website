One real ticket. Every pass detail exposed.

Each direction starts from the same existing ticket, preserves its identity and QR by default, allows complete PassKit overrides, and sends the resulting signed pass through a fixed email template.

A. Control room

All editing groups stay one click away while the pass and send action remain visible.

Best for frequent debugging and fast comparison across categories. It is the densest option and assumes the admin understands PassKit.

The manifest is the interface: reorder, relabel, and inspect every field in one dense table.

Best for exact front and back field work, localization, and finding provenance. Appearance and delivery feel secondary to the manifest.
