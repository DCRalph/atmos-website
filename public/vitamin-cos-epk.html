Your daily dose

Vitamin Cos is a DJ and promoter currently based in Aotearoa/New Zealand, and her mixing is every bit as vibrant as her personality.

Inspired by underground club scenes around the world, she builds all-inclusive, interactive dance floors, moving through global club, baile funk, hardgroove and house.

Opening 2026 with her second tour alongside Charlotte Plank, and debuting at Field Day and The Espy in Australia, Vitamin Cos is showing no sign of slowing down.

She has played across Asia, Australia and New Zealand, plus Spring Break Fiji. Her support slots include Sim0ne, Darude, Luude, Hermitude and Skream, and she has played Rhythm and Vines, Le Currents and the Laneway afterparty, to name a few.

Come get your daily dose and see her play.
