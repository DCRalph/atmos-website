Event overview: sell out profit, before costs and GST

Three layouts for the money block at the top of an event's Overview. In all three the sell out profit is always there (today it hides until a cost is entered), there's a before costs view, and GST moves out of the headline and the "Where the money went" card into its own card in the right rail. Same example event throughout: 240 sold, 380 on sale, $9,200 of costs.

The break even bar stays on top. Underneath, one table: so far and if it sells out down the side, before costs, costs and profit across. The sell out profit is the bottom right number, so it's the one the eye lands on.
