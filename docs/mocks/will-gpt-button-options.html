Will GPT button, round two

Five refinements of C, the quiet outline button with a glowing icon, taking cues from Google's Gemini motion. Hover any button to see its move. Dark and light, idle, hover and open.

What was borrowed from Google
  • The four point star rather than the lucide sparkles. Google's research found one sparkle is enough to say "AI".
  • Gradients with a sharp, nearly opaque leading edge that diffuses at the tail, used to point at what matters.
  • Motion with a defined start and end, played on an action, not forever. The one looping effect here only runs while the rail is open.
  • Everything animates on transform and opacity. The blur is drawn once and only moves or fades, so nothing repaints each frame.

1 TURN

The star turns 45 degrees and grows on hover, and stays turned while open. Motion: one 450ms ease on the icon, nothing else moves.
