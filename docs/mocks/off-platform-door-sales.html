Shared behavior

  • Actual money: record the amount collected, even if door prices differed from online prices.
  • Event reporting: include these tickets and takings in event sales, revenue and profit, with off-platform sales identified separately.
  • Historical tracking: attribute the sales to the event date and keep the recording date separately.
  • Already paid: saving records the takings. It does not charge anyone, issue tickets or send emails.
  • Accurate counts: add only sales missing from Atmos. Keep recorded sales separate from scanned admissions.
