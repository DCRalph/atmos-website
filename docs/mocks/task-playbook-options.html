Task playbooks: where they live and how you build one

Today playbooks sit at the bottom of the Task settings dialog, one tall form per task, and applying one gives no idea what dates it will create. All three options move them to a Playbooks tab on the Tasks page, list the starter playbooks beside your own, and show the real dates before anything is created. Same example throughout: the Club night starter, 19 tasks from 6 weeks out to 3 days after.

A Timeline with an edit panel B Inline table C Role lanes Apply shared

ATimeline with an edit panel

The playbook reads as a timeline counted back from the gig, grouped by week. Picking a task opens it in a panel on the right, with the same fields as today but laid out in one screen. The list stays readable while you edit; nothing else on the page moves.

BInline table

Every field is right there in the row, like a spreadsheet: day, task, owner, reviewer, what it waits for, and a critical flag. Rows sort themselves by day under the same week headings. Notes open from the row. Changes collect in a save bar at the bottom, so you can edit ten rows and save once.

CRole lanes

A lane per role across the weeks before the gig, a dot per task, so you see at a glance who is busy when. Pick a dot to edit it underneath, or drag it to change its day. The full list sits below the lanes.

AllApply to a gig, with the dates first

Shared by all three. Pick the gig and who fills each role (with what they already have due this week), and the dialog lists every task with its real date before anything is created. Dates that have already passed are called out, so applying a 6 week playbook 3 weeks out is a choice, not a surprise.
