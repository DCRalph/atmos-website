Decisions for you

  1. Which direction?I lean C for the offer, since it hands off to a page that looks the same, but A is the safest across inboxes.
  2. Just the refund emails, or every ticket email?All ticket emails share one layout (header, footer). Swapping in the real logo there updates tickets, comps and receipts too. I'd do the logo everywhere and the new panels only on the three refund emails, unless you want the lot.
  3. Outlook on Windows.It ignores rounded corners, so panels and buttons show square there. Everything else (Apple Mail, Gmail, Outlook on Mac and phones) shows them rounded.
