Declined payments: order timeline and analytics

Stripe records every try at paying, but the site only keeps how an order ended. These mocks use the four real declines from Club Vitamin. Buyer names, cards and order numbers are made up. The decline figures are real; the funnel counts and the email and naming times are illustrative.

What Stripe shows that the site doesn't

  1. Retried and paid on the same order (2). These orders just show "paid". The decline before it isn't recorded anywhere.
  2. Came back on a new order (2). The declined order stays "failed" with no email, so nothing links it to the order they paid on a couple of minutes later.
  3. One buyer paid $10.00 and never got a ticket. On 8 Oct, Apple Pay was declined, then accepted on another card 11 seconds later. Back then a "failed" order couldn't be paid, so it stayed failed with no tickets and no refund. The 13 Oct change fixed this for new orders, but this order still needs a refund or tickets issued by hand.

Already built on feat/payment-attempts: the webhook now saves every try (declines included) with card, method, Stripe's decline reason and billing email, and a backfill fills in past orders. The options below show how to display it.

2. Analytics

Ticketing › Analytics, filtered by the same event and date controls as the rest of the page. Each option can also show on an event's Overview for one event. Real figures for Club Vitamin: 40 orders tried to pay, 42 tries, 4 declined.

AA "Declined payments" card with a feed

Four headline numbers, why cards were declined, how buyers recovered, and a feed of recent declines. Each feed row opens the order.

Analytics · full-width card under the checkout journey

Why pick it: it answers "how many, why, did they come back, and who" in one card, and the feed doubles as the cross-order timeline.
  • A buyer who came back on a new order is matched by the same browser visit or the same billing email, for the same event.

BFolded into the checkout journey

No new card. The existing funnel gets a "Declined" step between hold and paid, with the bar split into first-try success and paid after a decline. One headline tile joins the stat strip.
