Live screen: a sales mode and a doors mode, made to be left on

Three directions for the event's live page, each shown in both modes, now dense: a dozen or more figures a screen rather than one. Sales mode has a live flow block, what's happening in checkout right now: who's on the page, who has tickets in a basket, who's holding tickets with the clock running, who just paid. Doors mode has the same for the door. Same example event throughout: capacity 400, 262 sold, net $8,310, doors at 8pm, 171 inside at 9:42pm. Frames are 16:9 and the type scales with the frame, so a TV and a laptop get the same layout.

What's the same in every option

The page

  • Its own full-screen route. /admin/events/[id]/live?mode=sales and ?mode=doors, rendered without the admin sidebar, header or rail, true black behind it. The same for organisers at /organiser/events/[id]/live. A small back link in the header corner, and a fullscreen button that uses the browser's fullscreen so a laptop plugged into a TV shows nothing but the page.
  • No scrolling on desktop. The layout is a grid that fills the viewport; charts and feeds take whatever height is left and feeds show as many rows as fit. Below about 900px it stacks and scrolls.
  • The mode is in the address, so a bookmark or a TV browser opens straight into it. Switching is the pills in the header, or the keys S and D.

Where the numbers come from

  • Doors is the existing 5-second live poll, extended with: inside by tier, door sales tonight, re-entries, ID checks, per-device rate over the last five minutes, and the expected final from past events' no-show rate.
  • Sales is the event overview and the sales curve, polled every 30 seconds, plus one new query for the last few minutes: visits that opened the page or added a ticket (the view and basket checkpoints), orders currently holding tickets and when the next hold expires, the approval queue, checkouts that expired in the last hour, and the latest paid orders.
  • "Typical checkout tonight" is the median from the checkout journey, for today's orders.

Animation

  • Everything moves on change, nothing loops. Numbers roll to the new value over about 600 ms. Bars and the flow ease to their new share. A new row slides in at the top of a feed with a highlight that fades over two seconds. On first load everything draws in once, staggered, the way the frames above do.
  • The "updated 3s ago" dot stays lit and blinks once per refresh rather than pulsing. If the data stops arriving for more than a minute it turns amber and the header says how old the numbers are.

Questions

Money on the screen? Net revenue and profit are on every Sales frame. If the screen will be somewhere door staff or the public can see, a "hide money" switch would blank those cells and the order amounts.
Auto-rotate? An "Auto" option that crossfades between Sales and Doors every 30 seconds, for a screen in the office during an event.
Window for "Right now". Ten minutes on the page and in a basket, five at the door. Both are a guess at what feels live without flickering to zero between sales; easy to change.
