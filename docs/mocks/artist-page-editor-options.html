Artist profiles

Edit the page itself

Bring back the block grid: 12 columns, drag to move, drag a corner to resize, and the themed layout (Headliner, Wall, Stage) keeps owning the hero. Three ways to edit it. All three edit the real page in place, so what you see is what visitors get, theme included.

A. Dock and side panel

Recommended

"Edit page" on your own profile turns the page into the editor. Column guides fade in under the blocks, a glass dock floats at the bottom, and the selected block opens its settings in a panel on the right. Theme changes repaint the page as you click.
